Sources & selection

Live publisher feeds are checked when opened and refreshed. The background research job reads original source pages and checks explicit event dates or primary-paper publication dates. It aims to gather at least 20 candidates, then ranks surprise (35%), consequence (30%), novelty (20%) and relevance (15%). Reports from the last 24 hours come first; up to 48 hours is used when needed. Prior stories are omitted unless a supported major new development changes them. Routine announcements and old research are excluded. Briefing text is an attributed source excerpt with known jargon expanded. Images are from the source article, when a suitable real image is available; known AI illustrations are omitted. The source-page research time is shown separately from the live-feed check. Fewer than five stories is okay.

THE GOOD ADDS UP

Your little collection

A calmer kind of dashboard.

Your morning and evening reset: a little perspective, a little movement, and something good to keep.

What works now

Exercise tracking and a streak for consecutive completed check-in windows, a personal good-moments journal, manual calendar events, school-break dates, running records, a separate no-YouTube streak, a daily Duolingo reminder, a daily vocabulary word, and a twice-daily quote, joke, and sourced fact.

Live connections

News from BBC, NPR, Ars Technica, The Verge, science publishers and college reporting, public Google Sheets work when their providers can be reached. The downloaded dashboard uses a public-feed service for news and Google’s own API for the Sheet; those services can have delays or outages. Failures and old data are labeled. News selection uses automated weighted scoring and source-date checks. It can miss good stories; shortfalls are shown without filler.

About the schedule

A new check-in opens at 7 a.m. and 7 p.m. Eastern. An open page checks news and your Sheet within 30 seconds of each 7 AM / 7 PM reset; background source research may take a few minutes to finish. Colors switch to dark mode at 7 PM and back to light at 7 AM; reopening catches up. Exercise gets a fresh window. Your journal is kept and the quote, joke, rabbit hole, and soft color palette shuffle at each check-in. Vocabulary changes daily. Running and no-YouTube logs are kept. The server also checks feeds while running. This local version does not send notifications or run when its host is stopped. A hosted deployment is needed for always-on access.

Your privacy

The Duolingo reminder is self-reported and links to your profile; it does not read your Duolingo account. Personal entries stay in this browser’s local storage. No account, analytics, or cloud sync is included. Use Settings to back up your data.
